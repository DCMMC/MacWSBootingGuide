Chrome 150 IOGPUMTLEvent destruction selector — 2026-07-29
================================================================

Scope
-----

This closes the repeated `IOConnectCallMethod` selector 0x19 failures seen
during the current broad-stable Google Chrome 150.0.7871.187 WebGL2 timer-query
workload.  The change is a cross-version IOGPU ABI translation.  It does not
force an IOReturn, skip an Objective-C destructor, or replace an event object.


macOS 13.4 producer (RE-confirmed)
---------------------------------

The probe resolved the live macOS framework image and method:

  image UUID: CE2B5551-857F-3EDD-9E4F-435215CC8C27
  image: /System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/IOGPU.framework/Versions/A/IOGPU
  -[IOGPUMTLEvent dealloc]: IOGPU+0x15488

The relevant live bytes are:

  eventDeallocCode +0x020: 68 12 40 b9 a8 01 00 34 a8 83 1e f8 00 14 40 b9
  eventDeallocCode +0x030: ff 7f 00 a9 a2 63 00 d1 21 03 80 52 23 00 80 52
  eventDeallocCode +0x040: 04 00 80 d2 05 00 80 d2 06 00 80 d2 07 00 80 d2

The decoded external-method setup loads object+0x10, places it on the stack as
the only scalar input, and executes:

  sub x2, x29, #0x18
  mov w1, #0x19
  mov w3, #0x1
  mov x4, #0
  mov x5, #0
  mov x6, #0
  mov x7, #0
  bl  IOConnectCallMethod

Thus macOS selector 0x19 is the destructor paired with the already proven
IOGPUMTLEvent constructor selector 0x18.


iOS 16.3 producer (runtime- and RE-confirmed)
------------------------------------------------

The iOS-native probe resolved the live method at IOGPU+0x17190:

  METAL_SOURCE_PROBE eventDeallocIMP class=IOGPUMTLEvent imp=0x203aed190 image=/System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/IOGPU.framework/IOGPU imageBase=0x203ad6000 symbol=<redacted> symbolBase=0x203aed190

Its corresponding bytes are:

  eventDeallocCode +0x020: 68 12 40 b9 a8 01 00 34 a8 83 1e f8 00 14 40 b9
  eventDeallocCode +0x030: ff 7f 00 a9 a2 63 00 d1 a1 02 80 52 23 00 80 52
  eventDeallocCode +0x040: 04 00 80 d2 05 00 80 d2 06 00 80 d2 07 00 80 d2

The ABI is identical to the macOS producer except that `a1 02 80 52` decodes
to `mov w1, #0x15`.  The on-disk iOS 16.3.1 symbol image independently names
the method `-[IOGPUMTLEvent dealloc]` at static 0x1eec71190 and its full
`otool -tvV` disassembly shows the same selector 0x15 and one-scalar input.

The coherent translation is therefore:

  macOS event create  0x18 -> iOS 0x14
  macOS event destroy 0x19 -> iOS 0x15

Test-control limitation
-----------------------

The run initially started the repository's experimental Retina VNC path, but
another task bypassed the active `macws_test_lease` and directly restarted
WindowServer with its own VS Code/no-VNC configuration.  The lease did reject
that task's scripted `macos_gui.sh stop`; it cannot guard direct launchctl
operations.  Chrome's fixed-selector CDP and GPU-query results above were
captured after the restart from one fixed Chrome PID, and their log slice is
self-contained.  A later 1194x834 VNC capture was deliberately excluded from
the evidence.  The same exact Chrome 150 binary's earlier controlled
2388x1668 Retina VNC and input result remains recorded in
`chrome150-secondary-partitionalloc-20260729.txt`.

This remains a bounded compatibility regression.  It does not promote the
separate experimental WindowServer command/completion adapters to production
protocol fixes, and it does not claim that Chrome's rAF/presentation pacing is
now fast; the measured median frame interval was still 78.5 ms.
