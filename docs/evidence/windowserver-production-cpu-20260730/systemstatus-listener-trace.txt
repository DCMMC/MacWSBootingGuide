Runtime capture from a direct macOS 13 systemstatusd launch with
MACWS_XPC_DEBUG=1.  This proves the server listeners enter the same
xpc_connection_create_mach_service hook as clients; flags=0x1 is the listener
flag.  It is why one exact-name mapping in libmachook can rewrite both ends.

#### XPC_TRACE mach_service create: 'com.apple.systemstatus' flags=0x1
#### XPC_TRACE mach_service create: 'com.apple.systemstatus.publisher' flags=0x1
#### XPC_TRACE mach_service create: 'com.apple.systemstatus.activityattribution' flags=0x1
